About The Club

Vikram Sports Kadgaon

Founded in 2018 in Kadgaon, Vikram Sports has grown from a local neighborhood XI into one of the most respected and feared cricket clubs in the region. We pride ourselves on nurturing raw talent, maintaining absolute discipline on and off the field, and playing aggressive yet sportsmanlike cricket.
